In this problem set, we will consider the QED process
$$e^- + e^+ \to e^- + e^+$$
which is known as Bhabha scattering.  We will label the particles as:
$$a + b \to 1 + 2$$
so e.g. the final state $e^+$ is particle 2.
We will consider the limit that $m_e \ll E$.  In this limit, the
helicity eigenstates for particles are given by:
$$\def\arraystretch{1.7}
u^\uparrow = \sqrt{E} \begin{pmatrix} c  \\ s e^{i\phi} \\ c \\ s e^{i\phi} \end{pmatrix}, \hspace{1cm}
u^\downarrow = \sqrt{E} \begin{pmatrix} -s \\ c e^{i\phi} \\ s \\ -c e^{i\phi} \end{pmatrix},
$$
and the helicity eigenstates for anti-particles are given by:
$$\def\arraystretch{1.7}
v^\uparrow = \sqrt{E} \begin{pmatrix} s \\ -c e^{i\phi} \\ -s \\ c e^{i\phi} \end{pmatrix}, \hspace{1cm}
v^\downarrow = \sqrt{E} \begin{pmatrix} c \\  s e^{i\phi} \\  c \\ s e^{i\phi} \end{pmatrix},
$$
where in both cases:
$$c \equiv \cos \left( \frac{\theta}{2} \right), \hspace{1cm } s \equiv \sin \left( \frac{\theta}{2} \right).$$

\newpage

\noindent
{\bf Problem 1:}
Show that the Matrix element for this process is:
$$M = -\frac{g^2_e}{s} (j_{ab})^\mu (j_{12})^\mu
-\frac{g^2_e}{t}(j_{a1})^\mu (j_{b2})^\mu$$ for suitably defined
currents $j_{ab}$, $j_{12}$, $j_{a1}$, $j_{b2}$ and with $s$ and $t$ the
Mandelstam variables.  Use a shorthand like $u(a)$, $v(2)$ for the
bispinors and notice that the helicity states have not been specified yet.\\[5pt]

\noindent
{\bf Problem 2:}
Why is there a contribution from both $s$ and $t$ channel, but no
contribution from $u$ channel?\\[5pt]

\noindent
{\bf Problem 3:}
Show that:
$$M_{RL \to RL} = -g^2_e  (1 + \cos \theta) \left(1 - \frac{s}{t} \right) = -g^2_e  (1 + \cos \theta) \left(1 + \frac{2}{1-\cos \theta} \right)$$
where $\theta$ is the angle between the initial and final state electrons.\\[5pt]

\noindent
{\bf Problem 4:}
Use Fermi's golden rule to calculate the scattering cross-section
$$\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}$$
for this process in the CMS with helicity $RL \to RL$.\\[5pt]
